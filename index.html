About

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Psychology at Shenzhen University, China. My research focuses on sensorimotor control and the cognitive mechanisms underlying perception and action. Using behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and neuroscience approaches, I study how humans perceive their body, make motor decisions, and adapt movements in uncertain environments.

I hold degrees in biomedical engineering and completed my postdoctoral training at Columbia University and Peking University. I have been leading my independent research program at Shenzhen University since 2025.

Research Interests
  • Motor Control
  • Motor Learning
  • Computational Biology
